# Cost of Capital

The weighted average cost of capital (WACC) is the average return a company must pay its providers of capital, weighted by how much of each it uses.

WACC = (E/V) x Re + (D/V) x Rd x (1 - T)

- E, D: market value of equity and debt; V = E + D
- Re: cost of equity
- Rd: cost of debt
- T: corporate tax rate (interest is tax-deductible, so debt gets a tax shield)

The cost of equity is commonly estimated with CAPM:

Re = risk-free rate + beta x equity risk premium

Beta measures how sensitive the stock is to movements in the overall market.
