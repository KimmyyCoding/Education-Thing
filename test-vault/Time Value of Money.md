# Time Value of Money

A dollar today is worth more than a dollar in the future, because today's dollar can be invested to earn a return.

Future value: FV = PV x (1 + r)^n
Present value: PV = FV / (1 + r)^n

where r is the rate per period and n is the number of periods.

Example: $1,000 received in three years, discounted at 8%, is worth 1000 / 1.08^3 = about $794 today.

Discounting is the reverse of compounding. The higher the rate or the longer the wait, the less a future amount is worth today.
