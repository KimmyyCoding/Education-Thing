# Depreciation

Depreciation spreads the cost of a long-lived asset over the periods that benefit from using it. Buying a $50,000 machine with a five-year life does not create a $50,000 expense in year one; under the straight-line method it creates a $10,000 expense each year.

Common methods:
- Straight-line: equal expense each year
- Declining balance: larger expense in early years
- Units of production: expense follows actual usage

Depreciation is a non-cash expense. It reduces reported profit but no cash leaves the business when it is recorded, because the cash left when the asset was bought.
