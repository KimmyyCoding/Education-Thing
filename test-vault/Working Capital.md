# Working Capital

Working capital is current assets minus current liabilities. It measures the resources tied up in running the business day to day.

The main components are inventory, accounts receivable and accounts payable. A growing company usually needs more working capital: it has to buy inventory and extend credit to customers before it collects from them.

The cash conversion cycle measures how long cash is tied up:

days inventory outstanding + days sales outstanding - days payables outstanding

A shorter cycle means the business funds itself more quickly. Some retailers have a negative cycle because they collect from customers before paying suppliers.
