# Revenue Recognition

Revenue is recognised when control of a good or service passes to the customer, in the amount the company expects to be entitled to. IFRS 15 sets out five steps:

1. Identify the contract with the customer
2. Identify the separate performance obligations
3. Determine the transaction price
4. Allocate the price to the performance obligations
5. Recognise revenue as each obligation is satisfied

Example: a software company sells a one-year subscription for $1,200 paid upfront. It receives the cash on day one but recognises $100 of revenue each month. The unearned portion sits on the balance sheet as deferred revenue, a liability.
